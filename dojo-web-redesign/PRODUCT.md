# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

2049 primarily serves an individual content operator who plans personal work and coordinates a small content team. The user works locally for long stretches and needs to move quickly between decisions, tasks, handoffs, and reviews without feeling like they are operating an enterprise admin system.

## Product Purpose

2049 turns content opportunities into coordinated execution. It helps the user collect and filter inspiration, convert useful findings into scripts and tasks, schedule personal work, hand work to teammates, monitor accounts and videos, make distribution decisions, and feed results back into the next cycle.

Success means the user can understand what matters, decide the next action, assign or schedule it, and later review the outcome without rebuilding context across separate tools.

## Positioning

2049 is a personal content-operations workbench with a shared execution core: inspiration, production, team handoff, publishing signals, account monitoring, video monitoring, distribution, and review all resolve into the same tasks and project context.

## Operating Context

- Daily planning and time scheduling for personal work.
- Project-based content production with team ownership and handoff states.
- Multi-platform inspiration retrieval through a future RapidAPI integration.
- Programmatic filtering followed by AI-assisted semantic filtering.
- Script-level analysis of selected inspiration for future video production.
- Account, video, matrix, and paid-distribution monitoring.
- Interactive BI views for trends, comparison, anomaly discovery, and drill-down.
- Weekly or cycle-based review that creates reusable operating rules.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Local-first delivery is the current scope; browser storage remains the persistence layer.
- Existing 2049 project, schedule, account, video, and imported data must remain usable.
- RapidAPI and AI provider credentials are not yet supplied. The local product may provide fixtures and explicit integration boundaries, but must not fabricate live retrieval or AI results.
- Visualizations should be used whenever they improve understanding or decision-making, and should support filtering, drill-down, comparison, and action creation.
- The interface is a bounded desktop workbench, not an infinite canvas and not a user-authored UI builder.
- Pages may use task-specific composition rather than a repeated dashboard card grid.

## Brand Commitments

- Product name: 2049.
- The desired character is friendly, capable, personal, and enjoyable for daily work.
- Superlist is the confirmed reference for workbench warmth, mixed task content, and a less corporate feeling.
- The product must avoid nested product shells, knowledge-editor aesthetics, and generic enterprise dashboard chrome.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing local project, schedule, account, ad-account, and video-monitoring data and fixtures in the repository.
- Existing Creator OS content flow, calendar, timeline, and review implementation.
- No verified external performance claims, customer evidence, or live RapidAPI/AI results are available and none should be invented.

## Product Principles

1. Every insight should be able to become a concrete action.
2. Personal work and team handoff share one task language.
3. Monitoring exists to support decisions, not merely to display metrics.
4. The interface stays calm by default and reveals operational depth when needed.
5. One object keeps its identity across inspiration, production, scheduling, monitoring, and review.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard navigation, visible focus, readable contrast, reduced-motion support, and responsive use on common desktop and mobile web sizes are required.
